Important limitations

Removal uses OpenCV's classical inpainting, not generative AI. It fills pixels based on nearby image content and cannot reliably recreate hidden embroidery stitches. Text touching dense stitching may leave visible smears or holes. The automatic detector may miss the green diagonal number or detect unrelated embroidered shapes. Test 2–3 images before running all 100.
